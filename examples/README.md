# Fictional example clients

The ten clients come unchanged from ImmuKnow-Template's current sample collection.
They help develop notice wording and layout; they are not vaccination schedules
or clinical assessments. Studio keeps descriptions and preview choices here,
while Template owns the source CSV and default notice assignments.

Each client is available for every maintained template and language. Their names,
addresses, history and supplied overdue facts do not change to fit a notice.
Some combinations intentionally produce applicability findings. Eight clients have recorded histories, including valid and invalid doses;
Marie Nicolas and Jennifer Sims have no recorded immunizations. Sarah Cook has
an explicit guardian choice. Angela Odom has the longest recorded history in
this collection.

Builds prepare facts through the reviewed native Core executable. Preview dates
are fixed in `previews.json`, after the latest supplied vaccination date. Editing
an imported CSV or configuration does not regenerate browser facts.

A complete project download includes these ten fictional source records and
their default assignments when **Include fictional client data** is selected.
Clear that option to supply your own records. Use the downloaded project's
native sample command to check its setup, then review the generated PDFs and
findings. Browser previews help design notices; they do not approve delivery.
